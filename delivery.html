Delivery Options

Getting your handmade chocolates to you safely and fresh.

🏪 Click & Collect

Select Click & Collect at checkout and we'll email you when your order is ready. Please wait for your confirmation email before visiting.

Collection hours: Mon–Sat 10am–4pm · Sun 11am–3pm

📦 UK Postal Delivery

Postage within the UK is a flat rate of £5.00 for orders up to 2kg. Sent via Royal Mail 1st Class within 3–5 working days. Weekends and bank holidays are not included. Busy seasons (Easter, Christmas) may cause slight delays.

UK delivery: £5.00 flat rate · Royal Mail 1st Class · 3–5 working days

🌍 International Shipping

We currently ship within the UK only. For international orders, please email us and we'll discuss options and pricing.

Email chocolinis@btinternet.com for international enquiries.
