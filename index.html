Our Story

Saltburn's very own chocolate factory

Nestled on Windsor Road in the charming seaside town of Saltburn-by-the-Sea, Chocolini's is a haven for chocolate lovers. We handcraft every single chocolate — from luxurious continental truffles to whimsical novelty shapes, personalised gifts, and indulgent dairy-free treats.

Step inside and you'll be greeted by the rich aroma of melting chocolate, a dazzling display of handmade creations, and the warmest welcome on the North Yorkshire coast.
