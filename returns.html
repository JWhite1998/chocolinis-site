Returns & Cancellations

Our policy on returns, refunds, and cancellations.

Chocolini's operates a returns policy for unwanted and faulty goods. Please contact us first via email or telephone.

Returns

Returns accepted with full refund if returned in original condition, unopened, within 14 days. Customers cover return postage. Send to: Chocolini's, 3 Windsor Road, Saltburn-by-the-Sea, TS12 1AX. Include your name, address and email.

Returns processed within 48 hours. Seasonal periods may take longer. Email chocolinis@btinternet.com with queries.

Faulty & Incorrect Goods

Contact us via email with order details, description and photo. We'll arrange a full refund and cover return postage.

Cancellations

Once payment is complete, cancellations cannot be made. Email chocolinis@btinternet.com with any queries.
