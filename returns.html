Returns & Cancellations

Our policy on returns, refunds and cancellations.

Should you need to return an item, please contact us first via email or telephone.

Returns

Returns are accepted with a full refund provided items are returned in their original condition and packaging, unopened. Customers are responsible for return postage costs.

All returns must be sent to Chocolini's, 3 Windsor Road, Saltburn-by-the-Sea, TS12 1AX within 14 days of purchase. Please include your name, address, and email.

Refunds are processed within 48 hours of receipt. During busy seasons this may take a little longer.

Faulty & Incorrect Goods

If your goods arrive faulty or incorrect, please email us with your order details, a description of the issue, and a photo if possible. We'll arrange a full refund and cover return postage.

Cancellations

Once payment is complete, cancellations cannot be made. Please email chocolinis@btinternet.com with any queries.
